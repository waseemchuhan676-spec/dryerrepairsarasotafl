Diagnosing the sound

Common Dryer Noises We Eliminate in Sarasota

Different sounds indicate specific failing parts. Here is how we pinpoint and eliminate your dryer noise:

01

Frigidaire Front-Load Loud Thumping Noise

Rhythmic thumping under load indicates flat-spotted drum rollers or a worn rear drum ball bearing cup. We replace the rear bearing assembly and install balanced OEM rollers.

02

GE Stacked Dryer Making Loud Noise & Vibration

Stacked washer-dryer units develop intense cabinet vibration when blower wheel squirrel cages loosen or motor shock isolators fatigue. We secure and realign stacked chassis mounts.

03

Bosch Dryer Cone Fell Out at Back of Drum

If the plastic center cone or air guide baffle breaks off inside a Bosch dryer, it clatters violently against clothes. We remount the rear air cone with heat-resistant factory hardware and inspect the rear bearing.

04

Midea Gas Dryer Noisy / Sock Stuck in Blower

Chattering or humming sounds often occur when socks, coins, or bra wires slip past the lint trap and jam inside the blower wheel. We safely disassemble the blower housing to clear obstructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my dryer making a high-pitched squeaking noise?

A continuous high-pitched squeak is almost always caused by worn drum support rollers or a dried-out idler pulley tension wheel. Replacing the rollers and pulley restores quiet operation immediately.

What causes a loud thumping sound in the dryer?

Thumping sounds are typically caused by flat spots on rubber drum rollers (especially after a heavy load has sat hot), a cracked drive belt, or worn rear drum glides and bearings.

Is it safe to run a dryer that is making grinding noises?

No. Metal-on-metal scraping or grinding indicates worn front drum glides or failing motor bearings. Continuing to run the machine can permanently gouge the metal drum or destroy the drive motor.

How much does it cost to fix a noisy dryer in Sarasota?

Dryer noise repair costs depend on whether the squeak or thump is caused by an idler pulley, roller wheels, glides, or blower wheel. We provide upfront, transparent quotes before any repair work begins with no hidden fees.
