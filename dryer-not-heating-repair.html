Diagnostic breakdown

Common Causes of Dryer Not Heating in Sarasota

Our Dryer Not Heating Repair Sarasota FL service pinpoints whether the failure is electrical, sensor-based, or mechanical:

01

Samsung Dryer Heating Element Replacement & HC Error

When a Samsung electric or gas dryer throws the HC error code, it indicates a failed heating element, bad heater relay on the control board, or tripped thermistor. We stock heavy-duty OEM heating coils for Samsung front-load dryers.

02

Maytag Dryer Bad Thermostat & Cycling Limiters

A bad Maytag cycling thermostat or blown high-limit cutoff causes the drum to spin endlessly without warmth. We test terminal resistance on Maytag Bravos and commercial suites to replace weak thermostats.

03

Dryer Temp Sensor / Thermistor Failure

Faulty temperature sensors transmit incorrect high-temperature readings to the main PCB, causing the dryer to shut down heat prematurely. We calibrate and replace OEM thermistors on LG, GE, and Whirlpool.

04

Gas Dryer Solenoid Coils & Glow Igniters

If your Kenmore or Maytag gas dryer clicks but does not ignite, burned-out dual gas valve solenoids or a cracked silicon carbide igniter is preventing gas release. We restore safe gas combustion on the initial visit.
