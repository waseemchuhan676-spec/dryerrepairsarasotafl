Commercial laundry solutions

Common Commercial Dryer Issues We Resolve

Commercial dryers run 10 to 18 hours daily, placing immense mechanical and thermal strain on components. We resolve:

01

Speed Queen Commercial Dryer Repair

From failed motor centrifugal start switches and bad contactors to worn trunnion bearings and damaged lint trap cover parts, we carry heavy-duty Speed Queen OEM components for single and stack units.

02

Coin-Operated Maytag Start Switch & Coin Drop Jam

If quarters jam in the slide mechanism or the timer initiates without engaging the motor, our technicians calibrate optical drop sensors, test 120V/240V start switches, and replace broken actuators.

03

Commercial Double-Stack Dryer Loud Noise

High-decibel screeching, thumping, or motor grinding in stacked units is usually caused by disintegrated support roller bushings, unbalanced blower wheels, or loose motor mount brackets.

04

Commercial Dryer Not Drying (No Heat / Long Cycles)

When high-volume dryers tumble but leave wet loads, we test high-BTU gas burner valve coils, inspect pilot igniters, or replace commercial 208V/240V multi-coil electric heater banks.

Frequently asked questions

Commercial Dryer Repair FAQs

Do you service coin-operated and card-operated laundry equipment?

Yes. We diagnose electronic card readers, mechanical coin drops, microswitches, and mechanical timers on Maytag, Speed Queen, and Whirlpool commercial dryers.

Can you replace Speed Queen lint trap cover parts and exhaust screens?

Yes, we stock factory lint screens, cast metal cover frames, and magnetic door latches to keep your units operating within strict fire code guidelines.

Do you work with condo associations and property management companies?

Absolutely. We provide net-30 invoicing for verified accounts, digital inspection reports, and coordinated key-code or maintenance manager entry across Sarasota County.
