Pinpointing the failure

Why Your Dryer Drum Won't Spin

A stationary drum is caused by one of these four mechanical or electrical failures:

01

Snapped Multi-Rib Drive Belt

Age and heat dry out rubber belts until they snap mid-cycle. We install Kevlar-reinforced replacement belts.

02

Seized Drum Support Rollers

Worn axle bearings lock up solid, creating friction that halts the drum and burns through the rubber belt.

03

Collapsed Idler Pulley Assembly

The spring-loaded arm maintaining tension on the belt loses tension or freezes, causing the belt to slip off.

04

Broken Motor Start Switch

If the drive motor hums but fails to engage the drum shaft, internal start windings or centrifugal switches have failed.

Why does my dryer turn on and hum but the drum does not rotate?

If you hear the motor running or humming without drum movement, the rubber drive belt has snapped, the idler pulley has collapsed, or a support roller has seized in place, locking the drum.

How can I tell if my dryer drive belt is broken?

Open the door and try to spin the drum by hand. If the drum spins very easily with zero resistance or friction, the drive belt is almost certainly broken or off the pulley.

Can a seized drum roller burn out my drive motor?

Yes. When a drum roller seizes, the motor tries to turn the frozen drum against extreme friction. This causes the motor windings to overheat and trip the internal thermal overload switch, eventually destroying the motor.

How much does it cost to fix a dryer drum that won't spin in Sarasota?

Most drum rotation repairs range between $140 and $320, covering belt re-stringing, pulley alignment, roller axle replacement, and motor capacitor testing.
