How often should dryer vents be cleaned in Sarasota, FL?

In Florida's humid climate, lint compacts faster inside ducts. Homeowners should schedule professional dryer vent cleaning at least once a year, or twice a year for large households or frequent laundry use.

What are the warning signs of a clogged dryer vent?

Key signs include clothes taking more than one cycle to dry, the dryer exterior feeling excessively hot, a burning lint smell during operation, and the exterior vent damper flap failing to open when the dryer runs.

Can a clogged dryer vent cause a house fire?

Yes. According to the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), failure to clean dryer vents is the leading cause of residential dryer fires. Lint is extremely flammable and ignites when restricted airflow causes heat buildup.

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost in Sarasota?

Dryer vent cleaning costs depend on the length of the ductwork, exit location (wall vs. roof exit), and accessibility. We provide transparent upfront quotes before any work begins with zero hidden fees.
