Dryer Emergencies We Solve Immediately

If you experience any of these symptoms, shut off your dryer and call our dispatch desk right away:

01

Burning Lint or Plastic Odor

Indicates trapped lint touching the open heating coil or melting wire insulation. Immediate safety hazard.

02

Tripping Breakers or Sparks

A short circuit in the terminal block or a grounded heating element drawing dangerous amperage.

03

Gas Odor Near Dryer

Leaking gas shutoff valve, cracked flex line, or faulty burner coils releasing unburned gas.

04

Exterior Dangerously Hot to Touch

Thermal cut-off safety fuse failed or severe exhaust blockage causing explosive internal heat buildup.

What constitutes a dryer repair emergency?

Any burning odor, visible smoke, electrical sparks, buzzing sounds followed by a circuit breaker trip, or the smell of natural gas/propane constitutes an immediate safety emergency. Turn off power at the breaker and call dispatch.

How quickly can an emergency technician arrive in Sarasota?

Our live dispatch team routes local technicians on call throughout Sarasota County. In true emergencies, we prioritize arrival windows and often have a certified technician on site within 1 to 3 hours.

What should I do while waiting for the emergency repair technician?

1. Unplug the dryer or shut off the double-pole 240V breaker in your main electrical panel. 2. For gas dryers, shut off the gas valve behind the machine if safe to access. 3. Keep the dryer door closed and do not attempt to run additional cycles.

We provide transparent, upfront quotes before any work begins. We explain every recommended fix clearly with zero deceptive markups or hidden fees.
