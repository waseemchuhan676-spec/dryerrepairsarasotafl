Pinpointing the failure

Why Your Dryer Drum Won't Spin (Turning On But Not Tumbling)

When the motor hums or controls illuminate but the drum refuses to tumble, one of these drive train failures is present:

01

Samsung Front-Load Dryer Drum Not Turning

In Samsung front-loaders, the heavy-duty idler pulley bearing frequently seizes, causing the multi-rib belt to slip off the motor shaft. We replace the full Samsung idler pulley bracket assembly and test the drum motor.

02

Whirlpool Dryer Idler Arm & Pulley Repair

The tension spring arm maintaining belt pressure on Whirlpool and Kenmore dryers bends or loses tension with age. We install heavy-duty steel idler brackets with sealed-bearing nylon wheels for quiet, smooth drum rotation.

03

Dryer Motor Assembly & Centrifugal Switch Replacement

If the drive motor clicks and hums without spinning the drive shaft, the motor start winding or internal centrifugal switch has failed. We replace complete OEM motor assemblies with calibrated blower wheel attachments.

04

Seized Drum Support Rollers & Axle Bearings

Worn axle bearings lock up solid under high heat, creating intense friction that halts drum tumbling. We replace all four drum rollers and rear bearings as a balanced kit to guarantee long-term reliability.

Why does my dryer turn on and hum but the drum does not rotate?

If you hear the motor running or humming without drum movement, the rubber drive belt has snapped, the idler pulley has collapsed, or a support roller has seized in place, locking the drum.

How can I tell if my dryer drive belt is broken?

Open the door and try to spin the drum by hand. If the drum spins very easily with zero resistance or friction, the drive belt is almost certainly broken or off the pulley.

Can a seized drum roller burn out my drive motor?

Yes. When a drum roller seizes, the motor tries to turn the frozen drum against extreme friction. This causes the motor windings to overheat and trip the internal thermal overload switch, eventually destroying the motor.

How much does it cost to fix a dryer drum that won't spin in Sarasota?

Dryer drum repair costs depend on whether the issue is a snapped belt, collapsed idler pulley, worn rollers, or the drive motor. We provide honest, upfront diagnostic quotes before any work begins with no hidden fees.
